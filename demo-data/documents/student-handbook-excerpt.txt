STUDENT HANDBOOK EXCERPT — ACADEMIC INTEGRITY
Fictional University · Sample portfolio document

Page 1 — Academic Integrity
Students are expected to submit original work and to cite sources accurately.
Plagiarism includes presenting another person’s words or ideas as one’s own without attribution.
First offense: failing grade on the assignment and a written warning to the student.
Repeated violations may result in course failure, suspension, or dismissal as determined by the Academic Integrity Board.
Students may appeal findings within ten business days of written notice.

Page 2 — Collaboration Policy
Unless the syllabus states otherwise, exams and take-home assessments must be completed individually.
Authorized group work must identify all contributors.
Unauthorized sharing of exam questions or solutions is a violation of this policy.
